# Where & how to sell this MIDI pack

You're selling a **digital download** (a ~6 MB zip). Any platform that hosts paid
digital files works; below are the ones that fit MIDI packs, ranked for a first release.

> Reality check on the price: **2.99 CHF ≈ $3.30 / €3.10**. Most stores price in USD/EUR
> and take a fee per sale, so on a $3 sale you net roughly $2–2.85 depending on platform.
> Fine as an entry price to gather reviews; consider $9–19 for a "Vol. 2".

## Best first pick — Gumroad or Payhip (easiest, you keep most)
| Platform | Fee | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Payhip** | Free plan: **5%** /sale (no monthly) | Simplest, lowest take, EU-VAT handled, instant payouts. **Recommended start.** |
| **Gumroad** | **10% flat** | Dead-simple, huge reach, handles VAT/tax, discount codes, easy embeds |
| **Ko-fi Shop** | **0%** (Gold) / small fee free tier | Good if you already have a Ko-fi |

**Steps (Payhip/Gumroad are nearly identical):**
1. Create a free account → verify email → connect PayPal/Stripe for payouts.
2. "Add product" → **Digital product** → upload `EDM-MIDI-Essentials-Vol1.zip`.
3. Title + descriptions: paste from `LISTING-COPY.md`. Price: set 2.99 in your currency (or ~$3.29).
4. Upload cover art (use the brief in LISTING-COPY.md).
5. Add the EULA text (BUYER-LICENSE.txt) into the product's license/terms field.
6. Publish → copy the product link → share it.

## Music-producer marketplaces (more targeted buyers, stricter review)
| Platform | Notes |
|---|---|
| **BeatStars** | Huge beat/MIDI marketplace. Sells "MIDI kits". Free & Pro plans; small fee. Strong discovery. |
| **Sellfy** | Clean storefront for digital goods, ~monthly fee, no per-sale cut. |
| **ADSR Sounds / LANDR / Loopmasters / Producer Loops** | Curated — they **vet/approve** submissions and take a larger revenue split, but bring an audience. Apply as a content provider; expect a review wait. |
| **Etsy** | People do sell MIDI/digital music tools here; per-listing + transaction fees. Good casual discovery. |

## Things to have ready before you list
- ✅ The zip (built — see `dist/EDM-MIDI-Essentials-Vol1.zip`)
- ✅ Title + short + long description (`LISTING-COPY.md`)
- ✅ License/EULA (`BUYER-LICENSE.txt`)
- ⬜ Cover image (1:1) — make in Canva from the brief
- ⬜ Optional: a short demo audio/video (render 3–4 templates to audio in your DAW) — biggest conversion booster
- ⬜ Payout account (PayPal or Stripe)

## A note on what you can and can't claim in marketing
- ✅ "Royalty-free", "1,320 MIDI files", "12 genres", "works in any DAW" — all true.
- ✅ Selling MIDI of common chord progressions/scales is fine — those aren't copyrightable.
- ⚠️ Don't claim it contains **samples/audio** — it's MIDI only. Say so clearly to avoid refunds.
- ⚠️ Don't imply endorsement by any artist/label or use trademarked names ("Serum", artist names)
  in the title; describing genre/"in the style of" in the body is fine.
