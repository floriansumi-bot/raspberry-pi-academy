## How to legally build a personal sample library
- **CC0** = public domain: use/modify/commercial/even resell, no attribution. Safest.
- **CC-BY** = free commercial use **but you must credit** the author.
- **CC-BY-NC** = **no commercial use** — personal/learning only. Avoid for releases.
- **Royalty-free (vendor)** = use the sounds *in your music* freely, but you **cannot redistribute/repackage/resell the raw sounds** (the Goldbaby & Philharmonia "not as-is" clauses are the model — nearly all vendor packs mean this).
- **Looperman-style host disclaimers** = legally weaker than CC0; fine for personal/most commercial use, do diligence for high-stakes releases.
- **The one rule:** open the license file inside every pack and keep it with the samples. Per-pack terms always win over a website summary.
